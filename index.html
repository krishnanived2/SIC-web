Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries regarding membership and activities.

Who can join Software Innovators Club (SIC)?
Any passionate student from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Kochi Campus who is enthusiastic about programming, tech development, and problem solving can apply to become a member.
Do I need prior advanced coding experience to join?
No! SIC welcomes beginners as well as experienced coders. We conduct foundational bootcamps and provide peer mentorship to help everyone skill up from scratch.
How often are workshops and events conducted?
We conduct regular weekly practice sessions, monthly hands-on workshops, internal hackathons, and guest lectures from industry professionals throughout the academic semester.
