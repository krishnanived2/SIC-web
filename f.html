About the Event

The event "Code Surge" was an exciting and intellectually stimulating coding competition organized by the Software Innovators Club (SIC) of the Department of Computer Science and IT. It provided an excellent platform for students to test their programming abilities, logical reasoning, and time management skills in a competitive setting. Hosted on the HackerRank platform, the contest encouraged participants to solve coding problems quickly and accurately, fostering an environment of focus, precision, and healthy competition.

Event Highlights

• Conducted on HackerRank with individual participation

• Separate challenge levels for juniors and seniors to ensure fairness

• Live leaderboard for real-time ranking and engagement

• Timed coding problems designed to test logic, problem-solving, and coding speed

• Strict anti-plagiarism measures to ensure fair play

• Encouraged quick thinking, precision, and adaptability under pressure

Event Structure

The competition consisted of a single one-hour round hosted online via HackerRank. Participants were required to:

• Solve coding challenges individually using their own HackerRank accounts.

• Complete problems designed according to their experience level (junior or senior).

• Compete for top leaderboard positions based on accuracy and submission speed.

The dynamic structure emphasized individual performance, time management, and strategic problem-solving.
