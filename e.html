About the Event

With 63 enthusiastic participants, teams of 4-5 members were formed randomly to foster collaboration, teamwork, and team-building skills. The event featured three meticulously crafted levels comprising puzzles, riddles, and crosswords, designed to enhance critical and logical thinking abilities.

Event Highlights

Participants exhibited remarkable engagement as they navigated the progressively challenging tasks.

Event Structure

The immersive storyline, combined with intellectually stimulating activities, provided an enriching and enjoyable experience, promoting both problem-solving and interpersonal skills.

Gallery

Organization

The event concluded with Team 9 emerging as the winners, demonstrating exceptional teamwork and analytical acumen.

Feedback

The winning team comprised students from BCA Data Science 2023 and BCom Taxation 2022.

Conclusion

The Escape Room was widely appreciated for its seamless integration of learning, collaboration, and entertainment, leaving a lasting impression on all involved.
