Overview:

A creative event where teams worked on funny/impractical startup ideas to test creativity, teamwork, and communication.

EVENT OVERVIEW

The event “Synergy Sprint“ was a creative and non-technical event

designed to evaluate participants’ creativity, critical thinking,

persuasion, and teamwork. Participants were provided with

deliberately absurd and impractical startup ideas by the coordinators,

from which teams randomly selected one idea to work on

EVENT HIGHLIGHTS

• Absurd startup ideas were provided and randomly assigned to teams.

• All branding and pitching activities were completed during the event.

• Strong emphasis on teamwork, creativity, and task distribution.

EVENT STRUCTURE

The event was conducted in two rounds. In the first round, teams

developed a brand name, logo, and slogan based on the assigned idea. In

the second round, teams presented a pitch using the same branding

elements, focusing on clarity, persuasion, and teamwork

ORGANIZATION TEAM

The event was coordinated by two student coordinators with the

support of 10 volunteers, who assisted in event management,

coordination, and smooth execution throughout the event.

PARTICIPANT FEEDBACK

Participants found the event engaging and enjoyable, particularly

appreciating the opportunity to collaborate as a team and divide tasks

effectively.

CONCLUSION

“Synergy Sprint” successfully achieved its objective of promoting

creativity, teamwork, and effective communication among students. The

team from the MCA batch emerged as winners, receiving a prize for
