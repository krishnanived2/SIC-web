About the Event

On February 28, 2025, the Software Innovators Club(SIC) hosted Mind Spark, a multi-phase puzzle challenge that tested participants' observation, memory, and problem-solving skills. Open to UG and PG students across departments, the event saw enthusiastic participation, with teams of five randomly assigned to encourage collaboration.

Event Highlights

Participants engaged in a series of well-structured phases, solving puzzles, decoding clues, and completing tasks to unlock the final master key. The movie clip session was particularly well-received, setting the tone for the event. Random team assignments fostered collaboration among students from different departments and academic levels.

Event Structure

The winning team consisted of students from Int MCA 2021, BCom 2022, and BCA 2024, showcasing the event's ability to bring together diverse talents.

Organization

The event received positive feedback for its engaging and challenging nature. Participants appreciated the mix of observation, memory, and problem-solving tasks, as well as the opportunity to work in diverse teams

Feedback

The Mind Spark was highly competitive yet collaborative, with participants praising its balanced structure.

Conclusion

MindSpark successfully provided a platform for students to showcase their skills while having fun, fostering teamwork and collaboration. The Software Innovators Club looks forward to organizing more such events in the future.
