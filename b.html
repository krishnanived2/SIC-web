About the Event

The Treasure Hunt was a thrilling three-day technical event conducted as part of the Savishkaara 2k25. Designed to test participants' problem-solving, teamwork, and technical skills, the event saw enthusiastic participation from students across various departments. The competition was structured in multiple phases, gradually eliminating teams until the final treasure was found. Starting with 59 teams (118 students), the competition progressively narrowed down to 3 finalists through multiple challenging phases. The event culminated in an exciting finale where the winning team was awarded certificates and a ₹2000 cash prize in recognition of their outstanding performance.

Event Highlights

● Technical challenges (digital puzzles, code-breaking)

● Physical tasks (sports-based challenges)

● Mental games (memory tests, decoding tasks)

● Adventure elements (scavenger hunt, treasure search)

Event Structure

The event followed a progressive elimination format spanning three days: ● Day 1: Preliminary round with technical challenges ● Day 2: Intermediate rounds featuring puzzles and decoding tasks ● Day 3: Finals comprising physical and mental challenges

Organization

A dedicated four-member core team consisting of event coordinators and volunteer leads planned and executed all aspects of the competition. During the event days, this core team was ably supported by fifteen volunteers who ensured smooth operations across all venues.

Feedback

The Treasure Hunt was highly competitive yet collaborative, with participants praising its balanced structure. Feedback highlighted enhanced technical and problem-solving skills, with strong engagement throughout.

Conclusion

The Treasure Hunt successfully fostered teamwork and creative problem-solving skills while delivering an engaging competitive experience. This edition has set a strong foundation for future competitions to build upon.
