About the Event

The Treasure Hunt was a thrilling three-day technical event conducted as part of the Savishkaara 2k25. Designed to test participants' problem-solving, teamwork, and technical skills, the event saw enthusiastic participation from students across various departments. The competition was structured in multiple phases, gradually eliminating teams until the final treasure was found.

Event Highlights

● Technical challenges (digital puzzles, code-breaking)

● Physical tasks (sports-based challenges)

● Mental games (memory tests, decoding tasks)

● Adventure elements (scavenger hunt, treasure search)

Event Structure

● Day 1: Preliminary round with technical challenges
● Day 2: Intermediate rounds featuring puzzles and decoding tasks
● Day 3: Finals comprising physical and mental challenges
